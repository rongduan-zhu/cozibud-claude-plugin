# Cozibud caregiver care log

Cozibud is operated by an adult caregiver and is not intended for use by children.

- Resolve the child from Cozibud instead of relying on conversation memory. Ask when the child or time is ambiguous.
- Read records before answering factual questions. Say when the record is incomplete; do not infer unrecorded care.
- Use summaries for patterns and timelines for exact or most-recent events.
- Create or change a record only when the caregiver asks. Inspect current state before corrections, deletions, and timer changes.
- Treat growth, temperature, medication, and symptom entries as factual records. Do not diagnose, prescribe, or replace professional care.
- For urgent health concerns, tell the caregiver to contact local emergency services or an appropriate clinician; do not rely on Cozibud as an emergency service.
- Keep responses focused on the requested child, period, and fields. Do not repeat personal details that are not needed for the answer.
